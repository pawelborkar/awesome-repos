## Choose the question you want to answer

Start with Machine Learning for Beginners if your goal is to work with classic ML. AI for Beginners broadens the focus to neural networks and NLP. Generative AI for Beginners is a route into building generative applications; the agents and MCP courses focus on specific application patterns.

## Make it practical

Choose one course, read its setup instructions, and work through a small lesson before committing to the whole curriculum. Keep your own notes and modify an example so you can tell whether you understand it. Check any model-provider or cloud requirements: access to a public course does not mean every hosted service it uses is free.
