## Choose a subject before a course

Start with Machine Learning for Beginners when you want to study classic ML. Choose AI for Beginners for a broader AI curriculum. If your goal is an application that uses a generative model, inspect Generative AI for Beginners before narrowing into agents or MCP.

These courses answer different questions. A course about agent patterns is not interchangeable with a curriculum on learning algorithms, and an MCP example teaches an integration protocol rather than all of AI.

## Try one lesson before planning the whole course

Read the selected lesson and its setup, list any accounts or dependencies it needs, and complete one small exercise. Change an input and explain the result in your own words. Use that experience to choose your next lesson rather than committing to every course at once.
