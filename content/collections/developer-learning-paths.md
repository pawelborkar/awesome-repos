## Pick a destination before a resource

Developer Roadmap and DevOps Roadmap help organize subjects to explore. OSSU Computer Science offers a curriculum-shaped path, while Coding Interview University focuses on interview preparation. These goals overlap, but the best route depends on what you want to accomplish.

## Turn a path into a small project

Build your own X offers project ideas, and JavaScript Algorithms supplies implementations to study. Pick one concept from a roadmap, build a small example, and check what you can explain afterward. A long list can help you orient yourself; it does not require completing every item before making something useful.
