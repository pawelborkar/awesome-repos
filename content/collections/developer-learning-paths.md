## Pick a destination before a resource

A roadmap helps you choose subjects. A curriculum gives those subjects a longer study structure. Interview preparation and implementation projects have more specific goals. Choose the kind of help you need before deciding which list to follow.

Developer Roadmap and DevOps Roadmap organize topics. OSSU Computer Science offers a curriculum-shaped route, while Coding Interview University is a study plan for interview preparation. Build Your Own X and JavaScript Algorithms offer different ways to turn a topic into implementation practice.

## Turn a map into one observable step

Choose one concept and define a small result: a program you can explain, an algorithm you can reconstruct, or a deployment step you can reproduce. Use the roadmap to choose that next step rather than treating every item as a prerequisite for making anything.

Course providers, repository ownership, and linked material can change. Follow current source links and setup instructions. None of these learning paths guarantees an employment outcome.
