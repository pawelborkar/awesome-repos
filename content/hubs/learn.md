---
title: Learn something worth building
---
Start with a goal, then choose a resource that gives you a way to practise. A course provides a route through a subject; a reference helps when you already know what you need. Books and exercises reward a slower, more deliberate approach.

For a first pass, pick one topic and one resource. Read the prerequisites, complete a small example, and check whether you can explain the result without following along. Stars can help you find widely used material, but they cannot tell you whether a lesson matches your experience.
